Let $O$ be the centre of the circle.
$\bcz AB$ is a diameter of the circle.
$\thus O$ lies on $AB$.
Join $OC$ and $OD$. \begin{align*} \text{Radius of the circle}&=\frac{1}{2}\times 12\text{ cm}\\[1mm] &=6\text{ cm} \end{align*} $\bcz OC=OD=CD=6$ cm
$\thus\Delta OCD$ is an equilateral triangle.
$\thus\angle COD=60\degree\quad\propeq$

Method 1

Area of the shaded region
$=\text{area of the semicircle } ABCD-(\text{area of the sector }OCD-\text{area of }\Delta OCD)$
$=\left[\dfrac{1}{2}\times\pi\times6^2-\left(\pi\times6^2\times\dfrac{60\degree}{360\degree}-\dfrac{1}{2}\times6\times6\times\sin60\degree\right)\right]\text{cm}^2$
$=(12\pi+9\sqrt{3})\text{ cm}^2$

Method 2

\begin{aligned} \angle AOD+\angle COD+\angle BOC&=180\degree\quad\adj\\ \angle AOD+60\degree+\angle BOC&=180\degree\\ \angle AOD+\angle BOC&=120\degree \end{aligned} Area of the shaded region
$=\text{area of the sector } OAD+\text{area of the sector } OBC+\text{area of }\Delta OCD$
$=\left(\pi\times6^2\times\dfrac{\angle AOD}{360\degree}+\pi\times6^2\times\dfrac{\angle BOC}{360\degree}+\dfrac{1}{2}\times6\times6\times\sin60\degree\right)\text{cm}^2$
$=\left(\pi\times6^2\times\dfrac{\angle AOD+\angle BOC}{360\degree}+9\sqrt{3}\right)\text{cm}^2$
$=\left(\pi\times6^2\times\dfrac{120\degree}{360\degree}+9\sqrt{3}\right)\text{cm}^2$
$=(12\pi+9\sqrt{3})\text{ cm}^2$
Note: The area formula $A=\dfrac{1}{2}ab\sin C$ used to find the area of $\Delta OCD$ is in the Non-Foundation Topics.
The following is an alternative way to find the area of $\Delta OCD$ using knowledge in the Foundation Topics only:

$\bcz OC=OD$
$\thus OE$ is the perpendicular bisector of $CD$.
$\thus\begin{aligned}[t] CE&=\dfrac{1}{2}CD\\[1mm] &=\dfrac{1}{2}\times 6\text{ cm}\\[1mm] &=3\text{ cm} \end{aligned}$
In $\Delta OCE$,
\begin{aligned} \tan \angle OCD&=\dfrac{OE}{CE}\\[1mm] \tan 60\degree&=\dfrac{OE}{3\text{ cm}}\\ OE&=3\sqrt{3}\text{ cm} \end{aligned}
Area of $\Delta OCD\begin{aligned}[t] &=\dfrac{1}{2}\times CD\times OE\\[1mm] &=\dfrac{1}{2}\times 6\times 3\sqrt{3}\text{ cm}\\[1mm] &=9\sqrt{3}\text{ cm} \end{aligned}$
