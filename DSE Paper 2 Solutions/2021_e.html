\begin{align*} m(m-a)&=a(1-m)\\ m^2-am&=a-am\\ a&=m^2 \end{align*}

Alternative Solution

Let $m=4$.
For option A:
$a=m=4$
$m(m-a)=4(4-4)=0$
$a(1-m)=4(1-4)=-12$
$\thus m(m-a)\ne a(1-m)$
For option B:
$a=2m=2(4)=8$
$m(m-a)=4(4-8)=-16$
$a(1-m)=8(1-4)=-24$
$\thus m(m-a)\ne a(1-m)$
For option B:
$a=m^2=4^2=16$
$m(m-a)=4(4-16)=-48$
$a(1-m)=16(1-4)=-48$
$\thus m(m-a)= a(1-m)$
$\thus$ The answer is C.

Answer: D 49% Correct
Substitute $x=-2$ into the identity: \begin{align*} a(-2+3)+b[3(-2)+1]&=c(-2+2)\\ a-5b&=0\\ a&=5b\\ a:b&=5:1 \end{align*}

Alternative Solution

\begin{align*} a(x+3)+b(3x+1)&\equiv c(x+2)\\ ax+3a+3bx+b&\equiv cx+2c\\ (a+3b)x+3a+b&\equiv cx+2c \end{align*} Compare the coefficients of $x$:
$a+3b=c\quad$ ......(1)
Compare the constant terms:
$3a+b=2c\quad$ ......(2)

Method 1

Substitute (1) into (2): \begin{align*} 3a+b&=2(a+3b)\\ 3a+b&=2a+6b\\ a&=5b\\ a:b&=5:1 \end{align*}

Method 2

Let $c=1$. The equations become \begin{cases} a+3b=1\\ 3a+b=2 \end{cases} Solving (using program), $a=\dfrac{5}{8}$ and $b=\dfrac{1}{8}$.
\begin{aligned} \thus a:b&=\dfrac{5}{8}:\dfrac{1}{8}\\[1mm] &=5:1 \end{aligned}
