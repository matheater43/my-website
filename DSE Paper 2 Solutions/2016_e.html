Answer: A 81% Correct
$\begin{aligned} \frac{a}{x}+\frac{b}{y}&=3\\[1mm] ay+bx&=3xy\\ ay&=3xy-bx\\ ay&=x(3y-b)\\[1mm] x&=\frac{ay}{3y-b} \end{aligned}$

Alternative Solution 1

\begin{align*} \frac{a}{x}+\frac{b}{y}&=3\\[1mm] \frac{a}{x}&=3-\frac{b}{y}\\[1mm] \frac{a}{x}&=\frac{3y-b}{y}\\[1mm] \frac{x}{a}&=\frac{y}{3y-b}\\[1mm] x&=\frac{ay}{3y-b} \end{align*}

Alternative Solution 2

Let $a=1$, $b=2$ and $y=3$.
For option A:
$x=\dfrac{ay}{3y-b}=\dfrac{(1)(3)}{3(3)-2}=\dfrac{3}{7}$
$\dfrac{a}{x}+\dfrac{b}{y}=\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{3}{7}}+\dfrac{2}{3}=3$
$\thus$ The answer is A.
