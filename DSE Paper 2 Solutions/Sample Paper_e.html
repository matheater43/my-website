Answer: B
Since the two girls are next to each other, they can be treated as one unit. Together with the $5$ boys, there are $6$ units in total.

Number of permutations of $2$ girls $=2!$

\begin{aligned} \thus\text{The required probability}&=\frac{6!\times2!}{7!}\\[1mm] &=\frac{2}{7} \end{aligned}

Alternative Solution

Let A and B be the two girls.
If A is the first in the queue ($1$ out of $7$ possibilities), then B must be the second ($1$ out of $6$ possibilities).
If A is the last in the queue ($1$ out of $7$ possibilities), then B must be the second last ($1$ out of $6$ possibilities).
If A is neither the first nor the last in the queue ($5$ out of $7$ possibilities), then B must be immediately in front of or immediately behind A ($2$ out of $6$ possibilities).
\begin{align*} \thus\text{The required probability}&=\frac{1}{7}\times\frac{1}{6}+\frac{1}{7}\times\frac{1}{6}+\frac{5}{7}\times\frac{2}{6}\\[1mm] &=\frac{2}{7} \end{align*}
