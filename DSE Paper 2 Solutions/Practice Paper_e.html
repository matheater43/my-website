Question 2

Answer: C
\begin{align*} 3a+1&=3(b-2)\\ 3a+1&=3b-6\\ 3a+7&=3b\\ b&=a+\frac{7}{3} \end{align*}

Alternative Solution

Let $a=0$.
Then, $3a+1=3(0)+1=1$.
For option A:
$b=a+1=0+1=1$
$3(b-2)=3(1-2)=-3$
$\thus 3a+1\ne 3(b-2)$
For option B:
$b=a+3=0+3=3$
$3(b-2)=3(3-2)=3$
$\thus 3a+1\ne 3(b-2)$
For option C:
$b=a+\dfrac{7}{3}=0+\dfrac{7}{3}=\dfrac{7}{3}$
$3(b-2)=3\left(\dfrac{7}{3}-2\right)=1$
$\thus 3a+1= 3(b-2)$
$\thus$ The answer is C.
