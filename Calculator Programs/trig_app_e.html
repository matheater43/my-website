Program Logic

The FreqOn command ensures that old statistical data are wiped.
For a vector $\mathbf{a}=(a_1,a_2,a_3)$, its length $\Vert\mathbf a\Vert$ is: $$\Vert\mathbf a\Vert = \sqrt{a_1^2+a_2^2+a_3^2}$$ The dot product of 2 vectors $\mathbf{a}=(a_1,a_2,a_3)$ and $\mathbf{b}=(b_1,b_2,b_3)$ is: $$\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=a_1b_1+a_2b_2+a_3b_3$$ The dot product is also related to the angle $\theta$ between $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ by: \begin{align} \mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}&=\Vert\mathbf a\Vert \Vert\mathbf b\Vert \cos \theta\\[1mm] \cos \theta &=\dfrac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{\Vert\mathbf a\Vert \Vert\mathbf b\Vert}\\[1mm] \cos \theta &= \dfrac{a_1b_1+a_2b_2+a_3b_3}{\sqrt{a_1^2+a_2^2+a_3^2}\sqrt{b_1^2+b_2^2+b_3^2}}\quad\text{......(*)} \end{align} The cross product of 2 vectors $\mathbf{a}=(a_1,a_2,a_3)$ and $\mathbf{b}=(b_1,b_2,b_3)$ is: $$\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}=(a_2b_3-a_3b_2,a_3b_1-a_1b_3,a_1b_2-a_2b_1)$$ It is a vector perpendicular to both $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, i.e. a normal vector of the plane formed by $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$.

Angle between two lines

Since $D=\pi$ in both iterations, the two vectors are stored as $(\maxX,\maxY,n)$ and $(M,C,A)$.
The angle is found by (*) in the first display.

Angle between a line and a plane

Let $\theta$ be the angle between the line and the plane.
(*) is computed for the vector representing the line and the normal vector of the plane.
Case 1: The angle between the 2 vectors is an acute angle.
The angle in (*) is $90\degree - \theta$. From (*), \begin{align} \cos (90\degree - \theta) &= M \\ \sin \theta &= M\\ \theta &= \sin^{-1} M \end{align} Case 2: The angle between the 2 vectors is an obtuse angle.
The angle in (*) is $90\degree + \theta$. From (*), \begin{align} \cos (90\degree + \theta) &= M \\ -\sin \theta &= M\\ \sin \theta &= -M\\ \theta &= \sin^{-1} (-M)\quad\text{(Note: $M< 0$)} \end{align} Combining cases 1 and 2, we have $\theta = \sin^{-1} \vert M \vert$.
This is the second display.

Angle between two planes

(*) is computed for the 2 normal vectors of the planes.
The first display gives the required angle.
To ensure that the angle is computed correctly, the input order of the 2 vectors representing each plane must follow the right-hand rule.
