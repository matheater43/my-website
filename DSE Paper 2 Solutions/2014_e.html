\begin{align*} BE&=CD\quad\text{(opp. sides of // gram)}\\ &=16\text{ cm} \end{align*} Let $AB=AE=x$ cm.

$\bcz AB=AE$
$\thus \angle ABE=\angle AEB\quad\base$

$\thus BAD$ and $CAE$ are diagonals of the square $BCDE$.
$\thus$ Area of the pentagon $ABCDE\begin{aligned}[t] &=\dfrac{3}{4}\times\text{area of the square }BCDE\\[1mm] &=\dfrac{3}{4}\times16\times16\text{ cm}^2\\[1mm] &=192\text{ cm}^2 \end{aligned}$

\begin{align*} \angle ADB+\angle ABD+\angle BAD&=180\degree\quad\asum\\ \angle ADB+90\degree+\theta&=180\degree\\ \angle ADB&=90\degree-\theta \end{align*}

Alternative Solution
