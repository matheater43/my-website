\begin{align*} \frac{a+5b}{7a+2b}&=\frac{1}{b+3}\\[1mm] (a+5b)(b+3)&=7a+2b\\ ab+3a+5b^2+15b&=7a+2b\\ 5b^2+13b&=4a-ab\\ 5b^2+13b&=a(4-b)\\[1mm] a&=\frac{5b^2+13b}{4-b} \end{align*}

Alternative Solution 1

Let $b=1$.
Then, $\dfrac{1}{b+3}=\dfrac{1}{1+3}=\dfrac{1}{4}$.
For option A:
$a=\dfrac{4-b}{5b^2+13b}=\dfrac{4-1}{5(1)^2+13(1)}=\dfrac{3}{18}$
$\dfrac{a+5b}{7a+2b}=\dfrac{\dfrac{3}{18}+5(1)}{7\left(\dfrac{3}{18}\right)+2(1)}=\dfrac{31}{19}$
$\thus \dfrac{a+5b}{7a+2b}\ne \dfrac{1}{b+3}$
For option B:
$a=\dfrac{4+b}{5b^2+13b}=\dfrac{4+1}{5(1)^2+13(1)}=\dfrac{5}{18}$
$\dfrac{a+5b}{7a+2b}=\dfrac{\dfrac{5}{18}+5(1)}{7\left(\dfrac{5}{18}\right)+2(1)}=\dfrac{95}{71}$
$\thus \dfrac{a+5b}{7a+2b}\ne \dfrac{1}{b+3}$
For option C:
$a=\dfrac{5b^2+13b}{4-b}=\dfrac{5(1)^2+13(1)}{4-1}=\dfrac{18}{3}$
$\dfrac{a+5b}{7a+2b}=\dfrac{\dfrac{18}{3}+5(1)}{7\left(\dfrac{18}{3}\right)+2(1)}=\dfrac{1}{4}$
$\thus \dfrac{a+5b}{7a+2b}= \dfrac{1}{b+3}$
$\thus$ The answer is C.

Alternative Solution 2

Let $a+5b=1$ and $7a+2b=b+3$.
Solving (using program), $a=\dfrac{7}{17}$ and $b=\dfrac{2}{17}$.
For option A:
$\dfrac{4-b}{5b^2+13b}=\dfrac{4-\dfrac{2}{17}}{5\left(\dfrac{2}{17}\right)^2+13\left(\dfrac{2}{17}\right)}=\dfrac{17}{7}\ne a$
For option B:
$\dfrac{4-b}{5b^2+13b}=\dfrac{4-\dfrac{2}{17}}{5\left(\dfrac{2}{17}\right)^2+13\left(\dfrac{2}{17}\right)}=\dfrac{85}{33}\ne a$
For option C:
$\dfrac{5b^2+13b}{4-b}=\dfrac{5\left(\dfrac{2}{17}\right)^2+13\left(\dfrac{2}{17}\right)}{4-\dfrac{2}{17}}=\dfrac{7}{17}= a$
$\thus$ The answer is C.
