Linear Equations in Two Unknowns

Basic Information

Mode:
1 COMP
Memory:
46/50 Bytes
Usage:
Solve simultaneous linear equations of the form $\begin{cases}ax+by=c\\[1mm] dx+ey=f\end{cases}$

Program Code

? → A : ? → B : ? → C : ? → D : ? → X : ? → Y :
(CX − BY) ÷ (AX − BD) → X◢
B⁻¹C − AAns → Y
The parts highlighted in green can be omitted if the solutions need not be saved.

Program Logic

Consider the simultaneous equations \begin{cases} Ax+By=C\quad&\text{......(1)}\\[1mm] Dx+Xy=Y\quad&\text{......(2)} \end{cases} (1) $\times\, X$: $AXx+BXy=CX\quad$ ......(3)
(2) $\times\, B$: $BDx+BXy=BY\quad$ ......(4)
(3) $-$ (4): $\begin{aligned}[t] (AX-BD)x&=CX-BY\\[1mm] x&=\dfrac{CX-BY}{AX-BD} \end{aligned}$
From (1): $y=B^{-1}C-Ax$

Examples

Solve $\begin{cases}2x+3y=-4\\[1mm] 7x-4y=15\end{cases}$.

Example 2 DSE 2021 (I) Q5

For $\begin{cases}3x=y\\[1mm] 2(y-20)=x+20\end{cases}$ , find $x+y$.
We move the variables to the L.H.S. and the constants to the R.H.S. to get \begin{cases} 3x-y=0\\[1mm] -x+2y=20+2\times20\end{cases}
