Question 2

Answer: D 80% Correct
\begin{align*} \frac{\alpha}{1-x}&=\frac{\beta}{x}\\[1mm] \alpha x&=\beta-\beta x\\ \alpha x+\beta x&=\beta\\ x(\alpha+\beta)&=\beta\\[1mm] x&=\frac{\beta}{\alpha+\beta} \end{align*}

Alternative Solution

Let $\alpha=1$ and $\beta=2$.
For option A:
$x=\dfrac{\alpha}{\alpha-\beta}=\dfrac{1}{1-2}=-1$
$\dfrac{\alpha}{1-x}=\dfrac{1}{1-(-1)}=\dfrac{1}{2}$
$\dfrac{\beta}{x}=\dfrac{2}{-1}=-2$
$\thus \dfrac{\alpha}{1-x}\ne\dfrac{\beta}{x}$
For option B:
$x=\dfrac{\alpha}{\alpha+\beta}=\dfrac{1}{1+2}=\dfrac{1}{3}$
$\dfrac{\alpha}{1-x}=\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{3}}=\dfrac{3}{2}$
$\dfrac{\beta}{x}=\dfrac{2}{\dfrac{1}{3}}=6$
$\thus \dfrac{\alpha}{1-x}\ne\dfrac{\beta}{x}$
For option C:
$x=\dfrac{\beta}{\alpha-\beta}=\dfrac{2}{1-2}=-2$
$\dfrac{\alpha}{1-x}=\dfrac{1}{1-(-2)}=\dfrac{1}{3}$
$\dfrac{\beta}{x}=\dfrac{2}{-2}=-1$
$\thus \dfrac{\alpha}{1-x}\ne\dfrac{\beta}{x}$
(At this point, we know that the answer is D. Below is for checking only.)
For option D:
$x=\dfrac{\beta}{\alpha+\beta}=\dfrac{2}{1+2}=\dfrac{2}{3}$
$\dfrac{\alpha}{1-x}=\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{2}{3}}=3$
$\dfrac{\beta}{x}=\dfrac{2}{\dfrac{2}{3}}=3$
$\thus \dfrac{\alpha}{1-x}=\dfrac{\beta}{x}$
$\thus$ The answer is D.
