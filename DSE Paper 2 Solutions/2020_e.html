Question 2

Answer: C 85% Correct
\begin{align*} a(a+b)&=2(b-a)\\ a^2+ab&=2b-2a\\ a^2+2a&=2b-ab\\ a^2+2a&=b(2-a)\\[1mm] b&=\frac{a^2+2a}{2-a} \end{align*}

Alternative Solution

Let $a=1$.
For option A:
$b=\dfrac{a^2+a}{2+a}=\dfrac{1^2+1}{2+1}=\dfrac{2}{3}$
$a(a+b)=1\left(1+\dfrac{2}{3}\right)=\dfrac{5}{3}$
$2(b-a)=2\left(\dfrac{2}{3}-1\right)=-\dfrac{2}{3}$
$\thus a(a+b)\ne 2(b-a)$
For option B:
$b=\dfrac{a^2-2a}{2+a}=\dfrac{1^2-2(1)}{2+1}=-\dfrac{1}{3}$
$a(a+b)=1\left[1+\left(-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)\right]=\dfrac{2}{3}$
$2(b-a)=2\left(-\dfrac{1}{3}-1\right)=-\dfrac{8}{3}$
$\thus a(a+b)\ne 2(b-a)$
For option C:
$b=\dfrac{a^2+2a}{2-a}=\dfrac{1^2+2(1)}{2-1}=3$
$a(a+b)=1(1+3)=4$
$2(b-a)=2(3-1)=4$
$\thus a(a+b)= 2(b-a)$
$\thus$ The answer is C.
