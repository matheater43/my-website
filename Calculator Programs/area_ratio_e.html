Program Logic

$z$ lies on the straight line passing through $A$ and $B$
$\Leftrightarrow$ $z-A$ and $B-A$ are parallel
$\Leftrightarrow$ $z-A=k(B-A)$ for some real number $k$
$\Leftrightarrow$ $\dfrac{z-A}{B-A}$ is a real number
$\Leftrightarrow$ $\dfrac{z-A}{B-A}=\overline{\dfrac{z-A}{B-A}}\quad$ ......(*)
From (*), \begin{aligned} (z-A)(\overline{B}-\overline{A})&=(B-A)(\overline{z}-\overline{A})\\ (\overline{B}-\overline{A})z-(B-A)\overline{z}&=\overline{B}A-\overline{A}B\quad\text{ ......(1)} \end{aligned} Similarly, $z$ lies on the straight line passing through $C$ and $D$
$\Leftrightarrow$ $(\overline{D}-\overline{C})z-(D-C)\overline{z}=\overline{D}C-\overline{C}D\quad$ ......(2)
$(1) \times (D - C)$:
$(D-C)(\overline{B}-\overline{A})z-(D-C)(B-A)\overline{z}=(\overline{B}A-\overline{A}B)(D-C)\quad$ ......(3)
$(2) \times (B - A)$:
$(B-A)(\overline{D}-\overline{C})z-(D-C)(B-A)\overline{z}=(\overline{D}C-\overline{C}D)(B-A)\quad$ ......(4)
$(3) - (4)$: \begin{aligned} (D-C)(\overline{B}-\overline{A})z-(B-A)(\overline{D}-\overline{C})z&=(\overline{B}A-\overline{A}B)(D-C)-(\overline{D}C-\overline{C}D)(B-A)\\[1mm] z&=\dfrac{(\overline{B}A-\overline{A}B)(D-C)-(\overline{D}C-\overline{C}D)(B-A)}{(D-C)(\overline{B}-\overline{A})-(B-A)(\overline{D}-\overline{C})} \end{aligned} By substituting $X = B - A$ and $Y = D - C$, we have \begin{aligned} z&=\dfrac{[(\overline{A+X})A-\overline{A}(A+X)]Y-[(\overline{C+Y})C-\overline{C}(C+Y)]X}{Y\overline{X}-X\overline{Y}}\\[1mm] &=\dfrac{\overline{X}AY-\overline{A}XY-\overline{Y}CX+\overline{C}YX}{Y\overline{X}-X\overline{Y}}\\[1mm] &=\dfrac{AY\overline{X}-CX\overline{Y}-XY(\overline{A+C})}{Y\overline{X}-X\overline{Y}} \end{aligned}
The area of the $n$-sided polygon defined by the vertices $z_1 = (x_1, y_1), z_2 = (x_2, y_2), ..., z_n = (x_n, y_n)$ can be calculated using the shoelace formula:
$A=\dfrac{1}{2}\displaystyle\sum_{i\,=\,1}^n (x_iy_{i+1}-x_{i+1}y_i)\quad$ ......(**)
where $(x_{n+1}, y_{n+1}) = (x_1, y_1)$.
Now for $a=a_1+a_2 i$ and $b=b_1+b_2 i$, we have \begin{aligned} \text{Im}(a\overline{b})&=\text{Im}[(a_1+a_2 i)(b_1-b_2 i)]\\ &=a_2 b_1 - a_1 b_2\\ &=-(a_1 b_2 - a_2 b_1) \end{aligned} So (**) can be rewritten as
\begin{aligned}\displaystyle A&=\dfrac{1}{2}\sum_{i\,=\,1}^n [-\text{Im}(z_i \overline{z_{i+1}})]\\[1mm] &=\dfrac{1}{2}\sum_{i\,=\,1}^n \left(-\dfrac{z_i \overline{z_{i+1}}-\overline{z_i \overline{z_{i+1}}}}{2i}\right)\\[1mm] &=\dfrac{i}{4}\sum_{i\,=\,1}^n (z_i \overline{z_{i+1}}-\overline{z_i \overline{z_{i+1}}})\\[1mm] &=\dfrac{1}{4}\bigg\vert\sum_{i\,=\,1}^n (z_i \overline{z_{i+1}}-\overline{z_i \overline{z_{i+1}}})\bigg\vert\\[1mm] \end{aligned}
For the 2nd last line, since the area must be positive, the sum must be of the form $-bi$, where $b > 0$.
So taking the absolute value of the sum is equivalent to multiplying it by $i$. (e.g. $(-8i)i = 8 = |-8i|$)
Note: The coordinates of the vertices of a polygon must be inputted in clockwise/anticlockwise direction.
e.g. Do NOT input the coordinates of the vertices of $ABCD$ in this order: $A \rightarrow C \rightarrow B \rightarrow D$

Example 1

Let $A=(10,1),\ B(12,0),\ C=(0,0)$ and $D=(12,1)$. Find the coordinates of the point of intersection of $AB$ and $CD$.

$0$   EXE / input coordinates of $C$ in complex form

/ $\dfrac{72}{7} + \dfrac{6}{7}i$ coordinates of the point of intersection = $\left(\dfrac{72}{7}, \dfrac{6}{7}\right)$
