Alternative Solution

Let $a=9$ and $b=7$. \begin{align*} (a-b)(a^2+ab-b^2)&=(9-7)[9^2+(9)(7)-7^2]\\ &=190 \end{align*} For option A: \begin{align*} (a-b)^3&=(9-7)^3\\ &=8 \end{align*} For option B: \begin{align*} a^3-b^3&=9^3-7^3\\ &=386 \end{align*} For option C: \begin{align*} a^3-2ab^2+b^3&=9^3-2(9)(7)^2+7^3\\ &=190 \end{align*} $\thus$ The answer is C.
